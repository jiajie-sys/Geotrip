# Japan Travel Guide

## Best Season

Spring is suitable for cherry blossom viewing.

Autumn is suitable for maple leaf sightseeing.

Winter is good for skiing and hot spring trips.

## Transportation

Japan has an efficient public transportation system.

Major cities are connected by trains and subways.

JR Pass may be useful for long-distance travel.

## Tokyo

Tokyo is suitable for:

- shopping
- museums
- modern city experiences
- food exploration

Popular areas include:

- Shinjuku
- Shibuya
- Asakusa

## Kyoto

Kyoto is suitable for:

- temples
- traditional culture
- historical streets

Recommended places:

- Fushimi Inari Shrine
- Kiyomizu-dera Temple

## Budget

Travel costs include:

- accommodation
- transportation
- meals
- attraction tickets

Budget travelers can choose hostels and convenience store food.

## Food

Japanese food includes:

- sushi
- ramen
- tempura
- wagyu beef

## Safety

Japan is generally safe.

Travelers should pay attention to earthquake information.
