# Norway Travel Guide

## Best Season

Summer is suitable for hiking and fjord sightseeing.

Winter is suitable for Northern Lights viewing.

## Transportation

Self-driving is a popular way to explore Norwegian fjords.

Public transportation is convenient in major cities.

## Nature

Norway is famous for:

- fjords
- mountains
- northern lights

Popular destinations:

- Oslo
- Bergen
- Tromso

## Hiking

Popular hiking areas require:

- waterproof clothing
- hiking boots
- sufficient water

Weather changes quickly in mountain areas.

## Budget

Norway has high travel costs.

Major expenses:

- hotels
- restaurants
- transportation

## Safety

Always check weather conditions before hiking.
