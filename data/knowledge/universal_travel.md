# General Travel Knowledge

## Budget Planning

Travel budgets usually include:

- transportation
- accommodation
- food
- attractions
- emergency expenses


## Outdoor Activities

Outdoor travelers should prepare:

- suitable shoes
- weather protection clothing
- navigation tools


## Weather

Travel plans should consider:

- temperature
- rainfall
- daylight hours
- extreme weather


## Transportation

Choosing transportation depends on:

- destination size
- travel distance
- budget
- flexibility requirements


## Trip Planning

A good travel plan should balance:

- sightseeing
- rest time
- transportation time
- budget
